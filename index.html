Every episode a show starts with was checked one by one and allows playing inside other websites. "More episodes" buttons open that channel's full playlist, where an odd episode may be blocked — the TV skips those. Most episodes come from the owners' own channels — StarPlus, Star Bharat, Cartoon Network India, Disney India, Discovery Kids, Pokémon Asia Hindi, Sagar Pictures, Tilak, Green Gold and others. Shows marked "Fan upload" come from fans' channels and can disappear if YouTube takes them down.

A fan-made nostalgia index. All shows belong to their owners; nothing is hosted here.
